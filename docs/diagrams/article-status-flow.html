State machine

Article status flow

Research stops at brief_review. Approving the brief is what starts writing, so the decision lands before the expensive half. Research itself is not free: it makes a paid SERP request and extracts claims from what already ranks. A run only ever picks up topic_selected, researched, drafted and qa_passed; every other status waits on a person. Two routes reach verified: an information-gain run returning PASS, or a reviewer overriding a held article with a justification different from the one already stored.
